Type

Barlow Condensed

Display, italic 700 and 800: league names, counts, season labels, the wordmark. Body is Barlow 400 to 600. One family, two widths, so the page stays one voice.

Scale: wordmark 26, league name 28, season 24, body 15, meta 13. Numbers are tabular.

Rules

  • Red appears on action only. The card blade on hover and expand, Retry, focus rings. Never as decoration.
  • The slant is the brand move. Wordmark bar, card blade, sport tag. Nothing else skews.
  • Status has its own hue. Green for live, amber for sample data; neither competes with the accent.
  • Touch targets are 44px. Inputs, Retry, cards. The filter bar sticks on scroll.
  • Alternate name stays as a line even when blank, so cards in a row share a height.
